About UnitConverter.live

Everyday measurements, explained clearly.

Our purpose

UnitConverter.live focuses on one everyday task: converting inches to centimeters and centimeters to inches. Our homepage combines a live converter, fractional inputs, visible calculations, worked examples and a free printable reference chart. No signup is required.

How the tools are built

The converter uses the exact relationship 1 inch = 2.54 centimeters. It accepts decimals and common fractions, lets you select decimal precision, and shows the calculation behind the result. The printable chart and worksheet help you use those measurements away from a screen. Older tools remain available at their existing addresses, while the homepage concentrates on inches and centimeters.

Our principles

  • Clarity: show units and meaningful context.
  • Accuracy: use established formulas and identify estimates.
  • Privacy: process inputs locally whenever practical.
  • Accessibility: support mobile devices and readable layouts.

Feedback

If you find an error, please contact us with the tool, input, expected result, and result received.
