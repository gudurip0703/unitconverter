About UnitConverter.live

Practical calculations, explained clearly.

Our purpose

UnitConverter.live provides free browser-based tools for everyday measurement, health, finance, construction, time, and productivity questions. Our aim is to make common calculations quick without hiding the method behind the answer.

How the tools are built

Each tool combines a focused interface with formulas, unit relationships, assumptions, and usage notes. We test common inputs, boundary cases, and mobile layouts. Because standards, data sources, and browsers change, we review the site over time.

Our principles

  • Clarity: show units and meaningful context.
  • Accuracy: use established formulas and identify estimates.
  • Privacy: process inputs locally whenever practical.
  • Accessibility: support mobile devices and readable layouts.

Feedback

If you find an error, please contact us with the tool, input, expected result, and result received.
