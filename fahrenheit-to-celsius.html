How to convert degrees Fahrenheit to degrees Celsius

Subtract 32 from the Fahrenheit value, then multiply by 5/9. This page applies the required offset before the scale factor. Enter any number above and the result updates instantly. The calculator keeps additional precision internally and displays a readable result for everyday use.

Worked examples

  • 32°F = 0°C.
  • 68°F = 20°C.
  • 77°F = 25°C.
  • 212°F = 100°C.

When this conversion is useful

Fahrenheit is commonly used for U.S. weather and household temperatures. Celsius is used internationally and in most scientific contexts. Cooking temperatures may be approximate because ovens cycle around the selected setting.

Accuracy and rounding

Keep extra decimal places until the final step when the value will be used in another calculation. For regulated, engineering, medical or safety-critical work, follow the precision and reference standards required by that application.
