How to convert feet to meters

Multiply feet by 0.3048. One international foot equals exactly 0.3048 meter. Enter any number above and the result updates instantly. The calculator keeps additional precision internally and displays a readable result for everyday use.

When this conversion is useful

Feet remain common in U.S. construction, room dimensions and height measurements. Meters are standard for international plans and metric calculations. Decimal feet and feet-and-inches are different formats, so convert inches separately when necessary.

Accuracy and rounding

Keep extra decimal places until the final step when the value will be used in another calculation. For regulated, engineering, medical or safety-critical work, follow the precision and reference standards required by that application.
