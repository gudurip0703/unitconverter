Using fractional inches

For 5 1/2 inches, divide 1 by 2 and add 5 to get 5.5. Multiply by 2.54 to get 13.97 cm. For 5/8 inch, the exact result is 1.5875 cm, or 1.59 cm rounded to two decimal places.

Measuring furniture and screens

Convert each dimension separately: a 48 × 30 inch tabletop is 121.92 × 76.2 cm. Compare both dimensions with your available space and allow clearance. A screen size usually describes its diagonal, so a 27-inch screen has a 68.58 cm diagonal, not a 68.58 cm width.

For sewing and craft projects

Confirm whether measurements describe the body, the finished item or the cutting size. Conversion alone does not add seam allowances or ease. A pattern calling for 5/8 inch commonly uses about 1.6 cm; follow the pattern instructions.

How to convert inches to centimeters

Multiply inches by 2.54. One international inch equals exactly 2.54 centimeters. Enter any number above and the result updates instantly. The calculator keeps additional precision internally and displays a readable result for everyday use.

When this conversion is useful

Inches are common in U.S. product dimensions, screens and construction. Centimeters appear in metric specifications, clothing measurements and international product listings. Screen sizes measure the diagonal unless stated otherwise.

Accuracy and rounding

Keep extra decimal places until the final step when the value will be used in another calculation. For regulated, engineering, medical or safety-critical work, follow the precision and reference standards required by that application.
