How to convert length accurately

This length converter handles everyday metric and imperial measurements for building projects, travel distances, room dimensions, product sizes and school calculations. Enter a value, select the original unit and choose the unit you need. The result updates immediately, and the displayed equation helps you confirm that the correct direction was selected.

Length conversion method

The calculator converts the starting value to meters and then converts meters to the selected output unit. The underlying relationships are fixed: one inch is exactly 2.54 centimeters, one foot is exactly 0.3048 meters, one yard is exactly 0.9144 meters and one international mile is exactly 1,609.344 meters. Using a shared base unit avoids inconsistent results when converting between two non-metric units.

Worked examples

  • 6 feet to meters: 6 × 0.3048 = 1.8288 meters.
  • 10 kilometers to miles: 10,000 ÷ 1,609.344 = approximately 6.21371 miles.
  • 36 inches to centimeters: 36 × 2.54 = 91.44 centimeters.

Choosing the right unit

Millimeters are useful for small manufacturing dimensions and material thickness. Centimeters are common for household objects and body measurements. Meters work well for rooms, lots and short outdoor distances. Kilometers and miles are normally used for road travel. Inches, feet and yards remain common in U.S. construction and home improvement work.

Common mistakes to avoid

Do not confuse square units with length units: square feet measure area, while feet measure a single dimension. When measuring a room, convert the length and width separately before multiplying them for area. Also check whether a written value such as 5.6 feet means 5.6 decimal feet or 5 feet 6 inches; those are different measurements.

Accuracy and rounding

The calculator retains additional precision during calculation and rounds only the displayed result. For construction cuts, surveying or regulated work, follow the precision required by the project and verify the result against the applicable specification.
