How to convert pounds to kilograms

Multiply pounds by 0.45359237. One international avoirdupois pound equals exactly 0.45359237 kilogram. Enter any number above and the result updates instantly. The calculator keeps additional precision internally and displays a readable result for everyday use.

When this conversion is useful

Pounds appear on U.S. body-weight, luggage and shipping measurements, while kilograms are standard internationally. Confirm whether a shipping limit uses scale weight or dimensional weight.

Accuracy and rounding
