Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

1. Overview

This policy explains how UnitConverter.live handles information when you use our free tools.

2. Information you provide

If you email us, we receive the information in your message. Calculator inputs are generally processed in your browser and are not intentionally collected unless a tool clearly states otherwise.

3. Information collected automatically

Hosting, security, analytics, and advertising providers may process IP address, browser and device type, referring page, pages viewed, approximate location derived from IP, and timestamps.

4. Local browser storage

Some tools, including calendar or journal features, may store entries locally in your browser. Local data remains on that device unless you clear it.

5. Cookies, analytics, and advertising

We and our providers may use cookies or similar technologies. If Google advertising is enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies to serve and measure ads. You can manage personalized advertising through Google My Ad Center. Where required, we will request consent before using non-essential cookies.

6. Use and sharing

Information may be used to operate and secure the site, respond to messages, diagnose problems, understand aggregate usage, improve tools, comply with law, and display advertising. We do not sell personal information. Data may be processed by providers helping us operate the site.

7. Retention and security

We retain information only as reasonably needed and apply reasonable safeguards. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Your choices

You can block or delete cookies, clear locally stored tool data, adjust ad preferences, or contact us about a privacy request.

9. Children

This general-audience website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy and revise the date above. Contact admin@unitconverter.live with questions.
