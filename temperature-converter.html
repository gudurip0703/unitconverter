Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin conversion

Convert weather, cooking and scientific temperatures with transparent formulas and immediate results.

Common reference points

  • 0°C = 32°F = 273.15 K
  • 20°C = 68°F = 293.15 K
  • 100°C = 212°F = 373.15 K

Formulas

°F = (°C × 9/5) + 32. °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9. Kelvin = °C + 273.15.
